# Release notes

- Requires **React Native Paper 6** (`>=6.0.0-alpha.0 <7`). Paper 5 is not supported.
- Requires **react-native-reanimated** `>= 4.3.0`.
- `DatePickerInput` follows Paper 6 `TextInput` (`variant`, `supportingText`, `endAccessory`).
- Repo tooling moved to **Bun**, **Oxlint**, **Oxfmt**, and **Lefthook**.
